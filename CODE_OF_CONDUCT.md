# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Treat people respectfully in this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces maintained by this fork's owner.

- Keep discussion constructive and relevant to the project. Critique ideas and
  code without personal attacks.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post hateful or sexualized content.
- Do not publish someone else's private information without their permission.
- Respect the maintainer's decisions, availability, and
  [current contribution policy](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Reporting and moderation

For concerns about public project content, open an
[issue](https://github.com/kmplngj/Felucca/issues/new/choose) identifying the content
and explaining the concern, where doing so is safe. Do not repost abusive content
or disclose private information. If a report needs a private channel, request a
private contact without including the sensitive details, then wait for the
maintainer to arrange one.

You can also use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls to report
abuse to GitHub, particularly when contacting the maintainer is inappropriate or
unsafe. Those reports are handled by GitHub rather than by this project's maintainer.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or
restrict participation, depending on the circumstances. This is a personally
maintained project; responses depend on availability.

For suspected software vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
